# Second-Quantization

***All about the method of Second Quantization and its applications in fields.***

Present **in Chinese** for the very first version, as a LaTex code backup. 

The fundamental structure and physical insights comes from one of the famous and prestigious textbook series in theoretical physics

<<Курс теоретической физики>>, or _Course of Theoretical Physics_ in english, 

initiated by Lev Landau and in collaboration with his student Evgeny Lifshitz starting in the late 1930s, and written with other Soviet physicts after the traffic accident of Landau.

## General contents

* Conceptual foundation of the second quantization, emerged from the 1D harmonic oscillator.
* Second quantization formalism in non-relativistic statistical physics.
* Landau-Ginzburg second order phase transition theory and many-body dynamics.
* Superfluid and superconductor as condensed matter exemples.
* Path integral and partition functional as the core concept of the statistical behavior of fields.
* Spinors and its non-relativistic origins, from the non relativistic quantum dynamics to relativistic photon field.
* Classic electromagnetic radiation review.
* Quantum field theory for absolute zero temperature, quantum electrodynamics and standard model of particles.
* Thermal field in a heat bath, including both thermodynamical equilibrium and non-equilibrum system.
* Effective/conformal aspects of fields, emerged from the renormalization theory.
* Riemannian and symplectic geometry, 3+1 hamiltonian description of the gravitational field, and first analyse on the black holes.
* Quantum behavior of the gravitational fields, and a glance of the gravitational quantization.
* More in appendix, with
   * Effective turbulence field theory
   * Stochastic geometry and dynamical phase transition
   * Algebraic topology, fiber bundles, characteristic classes 
   * Topology of the Feynman diagrams
   * Black hole thermodynamics
   * AdS/CFT correspondence, Gravity/CMT duality
   * etc ...
   
Highly concentrated on statistical,topological and geometrical properties of fields, and the anomalies rooted from quantum flucuation.

Generally focus on the statistical field theory, and little calculation of the scattering sections, so if more applications are needed, please refer to the real QFT textbooks.

**Absolutely not for beginners !!**

## Prerequisits 

* Differential Geometry : Basic understanding of the language of manifold, tensors fields, covariant derivatives, exterior forms, symplectic forms, and a little fiber bundles.
* Free particle dynamics : Action, Lagrangian, Hamiltonian, least action principle, canonical equations, Hamilton-Jacobi theory, small vibrational dynamics.
* Classic field theory : Special relativity, field configuration space, Lagrangian density, Euler-Lagrange equation, electromagnetic field coupling with charges, electromagnetic wave analyse. *(General relativity is welcome)*
* Quantum physics ：Hermitian operaters, Dirac brackets, fermions and bosons, spinors, electromagnetic effects, semi-classic approximation (WKB).
* Statistical physics : Basic thermodynamics, first order phase transition, micro-canonical ensemble, a bit of density matrix, blackbody radiation, phenomenology of superfluidity and superconductivity.
* Group theory : Basic understanding of a group structure, the differential geometry language of Lie groups, a bit of SU(2) and SO(3) is enough.
